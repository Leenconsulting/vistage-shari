About me

A career spent building businesses, and advising the people who run them.

I started at Accenture, then spent 20 years as Managing Director of a boutique consulting firm, growing it from five people to 55 through to its sale.

In 2023 I set up Lexcen Consulting to get back to the work I enjoy most: sitting with boards and CEOs on the decisions that shape their organisations.

Along the way I watched a lot of brilliant, experienced consultants go out on their own and then struggle to compete with the big firms. Leen is my answer to that.

And I chair a Vistage group because I've seen what a good peer group can do. My father joined a CEO peer group 30 years ago and still catches up with them every quarter. That's the kind of connection I want to build here in Perth.
